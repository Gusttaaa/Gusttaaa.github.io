# Design System: Gustavo Fontenelle Belmiro, Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Orbit"**

A near-black page with cool pale text, very light type and hairline rules. The interface is kept quiet so that one thing can be strong: a three.js scene fixed behind the home page, in which a tilted ring of glass tiles carrying the owner's project screenshots turns around his black and white portrait. As the page scrolls, the camera moves to a new pose for each section. "The Orbit" names that object and nothing else; no part of the interface is themed after it.

The look was pinned by the owner: dark, minimal, his photo at the top, and visible animation with camera moves that orbit as the page scrolls. Minimal here means few elements and a lot of black, not little motion. Type is one family, Geist, at light weights and in sentence case; Geist Mono appears only in small uppercase labels, dates and status. The only chromatic colour in the interface is a green dot for a live project and for availability; project images bring the rest.

Density is low. Sections are separated by up to 184px of vertical space, large statements sit beside a narrow label column, and structure comes from 1px translucent white rules. Inner pages (the projects index and each case page) carry no 3D scene; they use the same type, rules, cards and scroll-linked image framing.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-black ground (#050607) with cool pale text; neutrals only, plus one green signal dot.
- One family: Geist Variable at weights 200 to 350 for display, 400 for reading; Geist Mono Variable for small uppercase labels only.
- Display type is sentence case with negative tracking; uppercase is reserved for small tracked labels and controls (1rem and under).
- Structure from 1px white hairlines at 10% and 20% opacity; one panel tone (#0d0f10) for cards.
- Pale filled rectangles with a 6px radius for actions; full pills for tags, filters and the language switch.
- One WebGL scene, home page only, masked away from the text and driven by scroll.
- All entrance and scroll motion is opt-in under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; without it the content is complete and still.

## Colors

Cool, almost achromatic neutrals on a near-black ground, with one green used as a signal and one salmon used for form errors.

### Primary
- **Live Green** (`live`, #4ade80): a 7px dot before the status of a project with a public link, and the 8px availability dot under the hero (with its expanding ping ring). Nothing else is green.

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`bg`, #050607): page background, footer, open mobile menu, the 3D scene's clear colour and fog, and the text colour on every pale fill (primary button, pressed chip, current language, selection, skip link).
- **Panel** (`panel`, #0d0f10): project cards, the case image frame before its image loads, and the send-failure notice.
- **Hairline** (`line`, white at 10%): rules between rows, the footer's top edge, the mobile menu's rows.
- **Divider** (`line-2`, white at 20%): the rule that opens a list or a block, outlines of pills, chips, the language switch, the menu button and the ghost button, field underlines, and the resting underline of text links.
- **Foreground** (`fg`, #f1f4f5): display type, titles, emphasised values, field text, hover and current states, the focus outline.
- **Text** (`text`, #c9d0d3): reading text, navigation at rest, section labels; also the fill of the primary button, the pressed chip, the current language and the text selection.
- **Muted** (`muted`, #8f989d): secondary text, mono metadata, the second and third lines of the hero title, service tabs at rest, the footer.
- **Placeholder** (`placeholder`, #7f888d): example text inside empty fields. Nothing else.
- **Scrollbar Thumb** (`scrollbar-thumb`, #2b3033): the scrollbar thumb, on a Ground track.
- **Hover White** (`button-hover`, #fff): the primary button's fill under hover. Nothing else is pure white.
- **Image Well** (`image-well`, #000): the surface behind a card image. Pure black is also the opaque stop of the 3D scene's fade mask, where it is a mask value and not a visible colour.
- **Card Shadow** (`card-shadow`, black at 90%): the colour of the single shadow in the system (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Top Scrim** (`top-scrim`, Ground at 70%): the middle stop of the gradient that fades page content out under the fixed top bar.

### Tertiary
- **Error Salmon** (`error`, #ff9a8f): invalid field underline and its message. Form errors only.

### Named Rules
**The One Green Rule.** Green means "this is reachable right now": a project with a working public link, or the owner's availability. It is always a dot of 8px or less, never text, a fill, a border or a hover colour.

**The Pale Fill Rule.** The chosen or primary thing is a Text-coloured fill with Ground-coloured type: the primary button, the pressed filter chip, the current language, the text selection. No hue is ever used for selection or emphasis.

**The Images Bring The Colour Rule.** Chromatic colour enters through project screenshots, in cards, case pages and on the 3D tiles. The portrait is black and white.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geist Variable (with system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Geist Variable
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono Variable (with ui-monospace, "SFMono-Regular", Consolas, monospace)

**Character:** One sans family carried from weight 200 to 600. Large type is thin, tight and in sentence case, so it reads as a quiet statement on black; small type is where weight, tracking and uppercase appear. The mono face marks metadata (section labels, dates, status, counts) and never carries a sentence. Both fonts are self-hosted through `@fontsource-variable`.

- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.55, Text; measures of 30 to 34em): reading text. **Small** (0.9375rem): pills, chips, captions, field errors, secondary lines, the footer.

- **Button** (600, 0.8125rem, tracking 0.14em, uppercase). The language switch, the menu button, the back link and "back to top" share the size and weight at 0.08 to 0.1em tracking; small pills are 0.8125rem at weight 400 in sentence case.
- **Label** (Geist Mono 500, 0.78rem, line-height 1.2, tracking 0.2em, uppercase, Text): the heading of a section or of a block on a case page.
- **Meta** (Geist Mono 500, 0.75rem, line-height 1.2 to 1.4, tracking 0.14em, uppercase, Muted): status, availability, definition terms, field labels, chip counts. Dates in the experience list use 0.08em tracking. 0.75rem is the smallest step of the ramp.

### Named Rules
**The Light Display Rule.** Anything set larger than body size is weight 200 to 350, sentence case, with negative tracking. Weight 500 and above belongs to type of 1.25rem and under.

**The Small Caps Only Rule.** Uppercase appears only at 1rem and under, always with positive tracking (0.08em to 0.2em). A heading, title or sentence is never set in capitals.

**The Mono Is Metadata Rule.** Geist Mono is used at 0.78rem and under for labels, dates, status and counts. It never sets a sentence, a title or a button.

**The Label Is The Heading Rule.** A mono label is the heading of its section (it is the h2), placed in the left columns beside the content or directly above a list. It is not a caption stacked over a second, larger heading.

## Layout

A centred container up to 1680px wide with a side padding of clamp(20px, 3.35vw, 48px). Home sections use a 12-column grid with a 24px gutter: the section label occupies columns 1 to 3 and the content columns 4 to 12. Two-part blocks use uneven columns: services and contact 5fr to 6fr with a clamp(32px, 10vw, 144px) gap, the case body 7fr to 4fr, the home project card 3fr image to 2fr text, an experience row 4fr title, 5fr text and a date column of at least 9.5em. The projects index is two equal columns with a clamp(20px, 2.4vw, 32px) gap; stats and facts are three columns, skills four.

The top bar is fixed and 80px high (64px in the stacked layout) with no border; a gradient from Ground to transparent extends 40px below it so content fades out under it. Anchors scroll to the bar's height. The hero fills the viewport (100svh) with its text aligned to the bottom left, and the text block's action row is capped at 42vw so it stays in the band the 3D ring does not reach.

Vertical rhythm: home sections have clamp(88px, 12.5vw, 184px) of padding above and below; inner pages start clamp(40px, 7vw, 96px) under the bar and end with clamp(88px, 11vw, 160px). Inside sections, steps come from one px scale (8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 40, 48, 56), with fluid clamps for the larger gaps between blocks. Ruled rows are padded 14px (facts), 22px (points, service tabs) or 28px (experience). Interactive targets are at least 44px high (buttons 48px).

One breakpoint changes the layout: below 900px wide, and also up to 1199px wide in portrait orientation, everything becomes a single column, the navigation collapses into a menu button, stacked project cards stop being sticky, and the 3D scene moves above the text. The same query is used inside the scene for its camera poses. Below 480px the skills list is one column; below 360px the language switch moves from the bar into the menu.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat, with tonal layering: Ground, then Panel for cards, with white hairlines for edges. Depth is otherwise supplied by the 3D scene (real perspective, fog in the Ground colour, reflections) and by motion, not by surface effects.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card stack** (`box-shadow: 0 -28px 60px -24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.9)`): on the home page's sticky project cards only. It falls upward, so a card arriving over the previous one separates from it. The vertical cards on the projects index have no shadow.
- **Ghost outline** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--line-2)`; `var(--fg)` on hover): the ghost button's 1px outline, drawn as an inset shadow.
- **Field focus** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0 0 var(--fg)`; `var(--error)` when invalid): thickens a focused field's underline to 2px.

### Named Rules
**The One Shadow Rule.** The only cast shadow is the card stack's. Nothing else floats; the other two box-shadows are 1px lines.

**The Covered Card Rule.** While the next card slides over it, a sticky card scales down by up to 5% from its top edge and dims by up to 55% brightness. This is driven by scroll position, applies only in the wide layout, and is off under reduced motion.

## Shapes

Four radii, each with one job: 6px on buttons and the skip link, 10px on cards, the case image frame and the send-failure notice, a full pill (999px) on tags, filter chips, the language switch and the menu button, and circles (50%) for the status dot, the availability dot and the spinner. The focus outline carries a 3px radius (6px on buttons). Fields have no radius: a field is a single underline.

Lines are 1px and white at 10% or 20% opacity. A list opens with the 20% rule and separates its rows with the 10% rule. Outlines on pills and controls are the 20% line. The only 2px strokes are the focus outline (2px Foreground, offset 4px) and the spinner. The 3D tiles repeat the same language in space: rounded 16:10 slabs with a thin bevelled metal edge.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** rectangle with a 6px radius, minimum height 48px, padding 0 24px, uppercase 0.8125rem at weight 600 with 0.14em tracking.
- **Primary:** Text-coloured fill with Ground-coloured type and no border. Hover: the fill turns pure white (0.25s). Active: scales to 0.98 (0.35s on the system easing).
- **Ghost:** transparent with Foreground type and a 1px Divider outline; hover turns the outline Foreground.
- **Disabled (sending):** 70% opacity, progress cursor, and a 14px spinner after the text.

### Text Links
- **Inline links** (contact channels, footer, external links on a case page): Foreground with a 1px Divider underline offset 0.3em; on hover the underline takes the text colour (0.25s).
- **"More" links and the back link:** uppercase 0.8125 to 0.875rem at weight 600 with a 16 to 18px line icon (stroke 1.6) that moves in its own direction on hover: right 6px, down 3px for a download, left 5px for back, up 4px for "back to top" (0.45s on the system easing).

### Pills and Chips
- **Pill (tag):** transparent, 1px Divider outline, full radius, Text colour, 0.9375rem, padding 7px 15px 8px. Small variant: 0.8125rem, 5px 12px 6px. Not interactive.
- **Filter chip:** the same outline at 44px high with padding 0 18px and a mono count at 75% opacity. Hover: outline and text turn Foreground. Pressed: Text-coloured fill, Ground-coloured type.

### Status Tag
- Mono meta text in Muted. For a live project the text is Text-coloured and preceded by a 7px Live Green dot. The availability line under the hero uses the same voice with an 8px dot that emits a ping ring every 2.6s.

### Cards / Containers
- **Project card:** Panel background, 10px radius, clipped. On the home page the image fills the left 3fr and the text the right 2fr, at a height of min(40rem, viewport minus bar minus 96px) and at least 34rem; cards are sticky, offset 14px each, with 40px between them. On the projects index and in the stacked layout the image sits on top at 16:10.
- **Contents:** title, a Muted 0.9375rem line with the area and the organisation, a summary (clamped to five lines on the home card), up to five small pills, and a footer with the status tag on the left and a primary button on the right. Internal padding clamp(24px, 3.3vw, 48px).
- **Image:** cover-fitted on a pure black well, resting at scale 1.04 and easing to 1.09 while the card is hovered (1.4s); the zoom is off under reduced motion.
- **Shadow:** card stack shadow on the home page only (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Case image frame:** 16:9 (3:2 stacked), 10px radius, Panel behind, with a Muted caption below. The image moves inside the frame with scroll, one shot per image: crane (a tall page travels vertically), truck (a wide image travels sideways) or push (scales toward a focus point, 1.1 by default).

### Ruled Lists
- **Experience row:** title and organisation, bullet points, and a right-aligned mono date, between Hairline rules; the list opens with a Divider rule.
- **Fact rows:** a 7.5em mono term and a Foreground value, 14px padding, Hairline rules.
- **Points:** Foreground text in the Point style with 22px padding.
- **Stats:** a thin number over a Divider rule with a one-line description beneath (max 17em). Numbers come from the résumé and count up once when they enter the viewport.

### Service Tabs
- A vertical tab list of three large Muted titles, each with a 22px outlined diamond mark, separated by Divider rules. Hover (mouse) or selection turns the title Foreground and fills the diamond's centre (scale 0 to 1, 0.6s). The panel on the right holds one Intro paragraph and a row of pills, which rise in sequence when the tab changes.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** no box. A mono meta label above, then transparent input with a 1px Divider underline, Foreground text at 1.125rem, padding 10px 0 12px, Placeholder-coloured example text. Textareas are at least 7.5em tall.
- **Hover:** underline turns Muted. **Focus:** underline turns Foreground and thickens to 2px; the caret is Foreground; no outline ring.
- **Error:** Error Salmon underline with a 0.9375rem Error Salmon message below.
- **Send failure:** a Panel notice with a Divider border and 10px radius holding a sentence and a ghost button that opens the same message as an email.

### Navigation
- **Top bar:** fixed, borderless, over a Ground-to-transparent scrim. Brand name on the left; on the right four uppercase links (Text at rest, Foreground on hover or when current) and the language switch. A 1px underline in the link's colour grows from the left on hover and stays for the current page or section (0.5s).
- **Language switch:** a pill outline with 3px padding holding two 42 by 32px options; the current one is a Text-coloured pill with Ground-coloured type, the other Muted (38px square in the stacked layout).
- **Stacked layout:** the links are replaced by a pill-outlined "Menu" button (44px). It opens a Ground panel under the bar with 1rem uppercase links on Hairline rules, plus the CV download; Escape closes it and returns focus to the button.
- **Footer:** Ground with a Hairline top edge, four columns (identity, pages, social links and CV, back to top with the copyright), Muted 0.9375rem text and Text-coloured links.

### The Orbit Scene (signature)
- **What it is:** one WebGL canvas (three.js, loaded after first paint) fixed behind the home page at `z-index: -1` and inert to the pointer. A ring of 14 rounded glass slabs (16:10, thin bevelled metal edge), tilted about 22 degrees, circles the portrait with a slight wave. Eight slabs show project screenshots on their outer face; six are plain glass, graded from pale to dark around the ring; every back face is glass. The portrait is a flat black and white cut-out that always faces the camera. Lighting is a dark studio environment of five pale light boxes plus one cool key light, exponential fog in the Ground colour, ACES tone mapping, and a faint additive halo around each slab.
- **Placement:** in the wide layout the scene is shifted right of centre and masked by a horizontal gradient (transparent to 26%, opaque from 48%), which keeps the left band clear for the title. In the stacked layout it is centred above the text and masked to fade out between 40% and 54% of the height.
- **Camera:** one pose per section, interpolated by scroll position with smoothstep and damped each frame. Hero: front, full opacity, portrait visible. About: from below looking up through the ring, 34% opacity. Services: from the side, 42%. Projects and experience: opacity 0 and the canvas hidden. Contact: top-down, 34%. The stacked layout pulls the camera back and dims those poses to 14 to 20%. The portrait fades out early in the first transition.
- **Other motion:** on load the camera arrives from 22% farther away while the ring closes in from 1.55 times its radius and unwinds 1.5 radians over 2.4s (exponential ease-out). The ring then turns on its own at about 0.07 radians per second, and the camera drifts with the mouse pointer (up to 0.5 units sideways, 0.28 vertically).
- **Reduced motion:** the scene holds its hero pose with no intro, no idle turn and no pointer drift, and fades out over the first 70% of a viewport of scroll.
- **Without WebGL:** the portrait appears as an ordinary image at the bottom of the hero (86% of its height, 14% from the right; centred at the top in the stacked layout). No stand-in for the ring is drawn.

### Motion
- **Easing:** one curve, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, for every transform and entrance. Colour and border changes run 0.25s on the default curve (0.4s for service tabs).
- **Hero entrance:** each title line rises from behind a mask (translateY 110% to 0, 1.3s, 110ms apart after 150ms); the sentence, actions and availability fade in over 1.2s after 0.75s; the top bar fades in over 1.2s.
- **Reveal on entry:** blocks rise 28px with a 6px blur and fade in (0.9 to 1.1s), 90ms apart for neighbours, once.
- **Scroll-linked:** the About statement lights word by word (unreached words stay at 38% opacity on wide screens, 50% below 1200px, to keep contrast); covered cards dim; case images pan or push; the scene's camera moves.
- **Page changes:** view transitions of 0.55s on the system easing, with the project image shared between its card and its case page.
- **Reduced motion:** every entrance, loop and scroll-linked effect above sits behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` or its JavaScript equivalent, so with reduced motion requested all content is present at full opacity, numbers show their final value, images hold their first framing, the ping and spinner do not animate, and view transitions do not animate. Hover and press transitions on controls remain; the card image zoom does not.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the ground at #050607 and build structure from 1px white rules at 10% and 20% opacity, with #0d0f10 as the only raised tone.
- **Do** set anything larger than body text in Geist at weight 200 to 350, sentence case, with negative tracking (-0.01em to -0.04em).
- **Do** keep uppercase for type of 1rem and under with 0.08em to 0.2em tracking, and Geist Mono for labels, dates, status and counts at 0.78rem and under.
- **Do** mark the primary or chosen thing with a Text-coloured fill and Ground-coloured type.
- **Do** use the radius that matches the part: 6px for buttons, 10px for cards and frames, a full pill for tags, chips and switches, none for fields.
- **Do** keep the green dot for a project that is reachable now and for availability; list a project whose site is down without the dot and without a link.
- **Do** let project screenshots and the black and white portrait be the only imagery, and take every number, date and role from the owner's résumé.
- **Do** put new entrance, loop and scroll-linked motion behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, on `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, and make sure the content is complete without it.
- **Do** keep a 2px Foreground focus outline (offset 4px) on every interactive element, and tap targets of at least 44px.
- **Do** keep text out of the scene's visible area: the scene is masked where the title sits and dimmed to 42% or less behind any section with reading text.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second accent or tint. Green is a dot of 8px or less; salmon is for form errors; hover, focus and selection are always neutral.
- **Don't** set a heading, title or sentence in capitals, in a condensed face, or at a weight above 350.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface, or use Geist Mono for sentences, titles or buttons.
- **Don't** build the page around a themed concept or metaphor; the owner rejected concept-driven design. The interface stays plain and the 3D scene is the single strong element.
- **Don't** add a second 3D scene, put the scene on inner pages, or let it show at full strength behind reading text.
- **Don't** add cast shadows beyond the card stack's, or glows, glass panels or gradient fills on interface surfaces. Gradients exist only as the top bar's scrim and the scene's fade mask; blur exists only in transit during a reveal.
- **Don't** box a form field or round it; a field is one underline.
- **Don't** stack a small label over a second, larger heading; a mono label is itself the section's heading.
- **Don't** show a number, a live status or a link that the résumé or a working URL cannot back, and don't invent testimonials or client logos.
